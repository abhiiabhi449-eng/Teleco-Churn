Welcome

This system predicts whether a telecom customer is likely to leave (churn), so a company can act before the customer is lost.

How it works

1. A model was trained on 7,043 past customers.
2. You enter a customer's details on the Predict page.
3. The details are sent to a Django API, which loads the saved model.
4. The model returns a churn risk percentage.

What we found

Month-to-month customers and new customers leave the most. Two-year contracts almost never leave.
